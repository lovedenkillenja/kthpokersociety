Poker is more than a card game.

It combines mathematics, psychology and decision-making under uncertainty, where analytical thinking meets competition.

KTH Poker Society is a student-led, non-profit association for students who share that interest. We want to be a place where beginners and experienced players alike can build their skills, exchange ideas and meet people who enjoy the same problems.

Through tournaments, workshops, guest lectures and social events, we want to connect the theory to real decisions at the table. We're also building partnerships with experienced players, professionals and organizations that care about strategy and analytical thinking.

What we're planning

  • Tournaments and poker nights

    Free-to-enter games for every level, from complete beginners to competitive play.

  • Workshops and hand analysis

    Sessions where we take real hands apart: ranges, pot odds, equity and the reasoning behind each decision.

  • Guest lectures

    Experienced players and people from industry on strategy, risk and decisions under uncertainty.

  • Social events

    Evenings to meet students from across KTH who enjoy thinking in probabilities.

Questions

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Membership is currently free, and so are our events.

Do you play for money?

No. We never take entry fees for games and nobody plays for stakes. Prizes, when there are any, come from partners.

Do I need to know how to play?

No. Beginners are welcome, and we'll go through the basics. Experienced players are just as welcome.

Who can become a member?

Any student at KTH. Others who share our interest in poker and strategy can join too.

How do I apply for a role?

Go to Open roles, press Apply and fill in the short application form. You don't need to be a member first.

Privacy notice

KTH Poker Society is a student-led, non-profit association in Stockholm. We're responsible for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: kthpokersociety@gmail.com.

What we store when you become a member

  • Your name and email address
  • Your program and year, if you give them
  • When you joined and when you agreed to this notice
  • Your password, stored encrypted by our provider. We can never see it.

Why we store it

To keep a register of our members and to send you information about the society and its events. The legal basis is your membership and your consent.

Where it's stored

Member accounts are stored with Supabase on servers in the EU. The website is hosted by Netlify. We don't sell or share your details with sponsors or anyone else.

How long we keep it

As long as you're a member. When you ask us to end your membership, we delete your account within 30 days.

Applications for roles

Applications are stored with Supabase. Only the recruitment team can read them, and they're deleted within two months after recruitment closes unless you're accepted.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time by writing to kthpokersociety@gmail.com. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
