      How to play & hand rankings

      1. Sit down (400–2,000 chips) Create a 2–6 seat room or enter a 5-letter code. Your buy-in moves from your balance to your table stack and returns when you leave.
      2. Act within 30 seconds Blinds are 10 / 20. Each player gets 2 private hole cards and shares 5 community cards across Preflop, Flop, Turn, and River. Timing out twice in a row sets you to Sitting out.
      3. Best 5-card hand wins Make the strongest 5-card combination from your 2 hole cards + 5 community cards. Keyboard shortcuts: F Fold · C Check/Call · R Raise · D Deal · ? Guide.
